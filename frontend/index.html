What's inside

Everything HR touches, in one system.

Six workflows, one login — built around how admins and employees actually move through a workday.

Attendance tracking

Daily and weekly views with check-in / check-out, and clear status: present, absent, half-day or leave.

Leave & time-off

Apply for paid, sick or unpaid leave with a date range and remarks, then track pending, approved or rejected status.

Payroll visibility

Employees get a clear, read-only view of their salary structure; admins keep full control over accuracy.

Approval workflows

Admins review requests, approve or reject with a comment, and changes reflect in employee records immediately.

Role-based access

Employees and HR officers each see exactly what their role needs — nothing more, nothing hidden.

Employee profiles

Personal details, job details, documents and profile photo — editable within the limits each role allows.

Built for two roles

One system, two very different days.

Dayflow's dashboard reshapes itself depending on who's signed in.

Employee

Comes in, checks status, moves on.

  • View personal profile, job details and documents
  • Check in / check out and view attendance history
  • Apply for leave and track its approval status
  • View salary structure — read-only
  • Edit limited fields like address, phone and photo
Admin / HR officer

Sees everyone, approves everything.

  • Manage the full employee list and switch between profiles
  • Review attendance records across the team
  • Approve or reject leave requests, with comments
  • Update salary structures and ensure payroll accuracy
  • Edit any employee's details when needed
